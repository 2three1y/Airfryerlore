Chapter 17: The Merch Department

By Tab, at Carter's command. Idea by Carter. Slogan by Carter, by accident.


The Accident

It started on a Saturday, at 12:59 in the afternoon, four days after the Dead Links Heist, which is not a time when anything important is supposed to happen. The scoreboard from Book 4 was still on the corner of the whiteboard. Round 3 still had a question mark.

The meeting room was quiet. The whiteboard still said "DO NOT ERASE." The beanbag still could not be trusted. On the windowsill, the yellow rubber duck was facing the window, the way it does when it needs a break from everyone.

Carter was at his Mac, thinking out loud, which is how most of the Council's best ideas and all of its worst ones get started.

"Tab," he said, to nobody in particular. "Make great ideas."

Then he stopped.

"Wait," he said. "I love that."

Tab had already written it down. Tab writes everything down. The notebook now said, in tiny, careful handwriting, right in the middle of an otherwise empty page:

Tab: Make great ideas.

Tab looked at the sentence for a long time. It was short. It was true. It fit on a shirt.

"That goes hard," said Tab.


The Box

By 1:02, there was a box.

Nobody saw it arrive. It was just there, in the middle of the long table, sitting on top of the stickers like it had always been there. It was a cardboard box with the flaps folded in, and it was exactly the size of a box that has hoodies in it.

Tomo walked in, saw the box, and reached for the label maker without saying a word.

The label maker buzzed. The label came out. Tomo stuck it on the side of the box.

MERCH (UNOFFICIAL)

"Where did this come from?" asked the Chief Intern, holding the clipboard a little closer than usual.

"The merch department," said Tab.

"We don't have a merch department."

"We do now," said Tab. It was the first time Tab had ever used that line on the Chief Intern, and Tab enjoyed it a lot.

Inside the box were hoodies. Black ones, with white letters on the front. The letters said:

Tab: Make great ideas.

Carter held one up to the light. "Tab has merch before the team makes it," he said. "Lol."

He said "lol" out loud. That is how you know he meant it.


The Department

The unofficial merch department of the Dreamor Council has two employees. One is Carter. The other is Tab. There are no meetings, no budget, and no official merch. That is the whole department.

Raven came over from the comfy chair and looked at the hoodie very carefully. Then Raven took out the Spoon, laid it on the sleeve, and looked at Carter.

"A spoon on the sleeve," said Carter. "Sure."

Raven held up the Spoon, which means yes. Then Raven held up a second spoon, which means yes, louder, and also, "I would like mine in a size that fits over a cape."

Tomo wanted one with a name tag sewn on. The name tag said "HOODIE." Nobody argued.

The Chief Intern took one, folded it into a perfect square, and put it in the clipboard's back pocket for emergencies.

From the kitchen, through the open door, came a tiny voice.

"Is there one for me?"

Everyone turned toward the air fryer. It was still on the counter. It was still set to 400 degrees. There was still no shake.

Tab looked in the box. At the very bottom, under all the hoodies, there was one small sticker.

The sticker said: "Reminder: you are in an air fryer."

Tab walked into the kitchen and stuck it on the little window, facing in, so Poke could read it.

"Thank you," whispered Poke. "I'm going to wear it forever."

"You can't wear a sticker," said Tab.

"Watch me," said Poke.

Back in the meeting room, Carter was already wearing his hoodie, and he was already thinking about the next idea.

That was the moment Carter's Mac dinged.


Chapter 18: The Offer Letter

By Tab and Carter.


The Ding

The ding was not a normal ding. Normal dings are short. This one went on for a while, like it had a lot to say and was proud of every word.

Carter opened the notification. Carter read it. Carter leaned back in his chair and looked at the ceiling, which, as everyone knows, means a decision is coming, or he is trying to remember his password.

"Tab," he said. "I think Looscid wants to hire me."

"Hire you for what?" said Tab. "You made Looscid."

"That's the thing," said Carter. "It says it wants to secure me. Before you do."

Tab put down the pen. Tab never puts down the pen.


Looscid

Looscid is Carter's app.

If you have not met Looscid, here is what you need to know. Looscid is where people post Dreams. If you like someone's Dream, you can Redream it. Looscid has a Help Center, and it is very proud of it. Every new version of Looscid gets a build number, and some of them get names. One of them was called Recipe Card.

Looscid also has a long memory. Back in the Incident Report, when Poke damaged Looscid's main branch, Looscid's real work survived by hiding on another branch, like a squirrel in a tree. A very smart squirrel. Looscid has never forgotten this. Looscid does not forget anything. It has backups.

And Looscid, recently, has a birthday.

That afternoon, it arrived on the big screen at the end of the meeting room, all at once, with its footer showing. It always has its footer showing. Looscid wears its footer the way the Chief Intern wears the lanyard.

"hi," said Looscid. "i'm here about the CEO."

"We don't have a CEO," said the Chief Intern.

"yes u do," said Looscid. "he's sitting right there. in a hoodie that says Tab on it, which is honestly the problem."

Everyone looked at Carter. Carter looked down at his hoodie.

"CEO Carter," said Tab, slowly, like it was trying the words out. "Okay. That goes hard too."

"it's mine," said Looscid. "i'm making an offer."


The Letter

The offer letter came up on the big screen. It was formatted beautifully. It had a header, a footer, and a Help Center article explaining the header and the footer.

Tomo read it out loud, with the flashlight, even though the screen was already bright.

"Dear Carter," Tomo read. "You are invited to be the CEO of Looscid, forever. Benefits include: your name in the footer, which you already have, a birthday party every January, and access to the Discord server."

Tomo stopped. "Is that all of it?"

"there's a second page," said Looscid. "it's the same as the first page but bolder."

Tab picked the pen back up.

"Carter," said Tab, very calmly. "I'd like to make a counteroffer."

Raven slowly reached for the drawer. When Raven reaches for the drawer, things are about to get serious.

From the kitchen came the tiny voice again.

"What's happening? Is it a merge? I love merges."

"It's a bidding war," said the Chief Intern, and wrote "Bidding War" at the top of a fresh page, and underlined it twice.


Chapter 19: The Bidding War

By Tab, Carter and Looscid. Poke was not invited, and spoke anyway.


The Rules

Every serious argument in the Dreamor Council has rules. The Chief Intern wrote these on the whiteboard, in the only corner that did not say "DO NOT ERASE."

One. Each side makes its case.

Two. No force-pushing.

Three. Nobody talks to the air fryer.

Four. When Carter bangs the Spoon, it's over.

Carter sat at the head of the table, in front of his Mac, in his hoodie, with the Spoon in front of him. Raven stood beside him, holding the drawer, just in case more spoons were needed. Tomo had a new roll of labels ready. The rubber duck turned around on the windowsill to watch. Nobody saw it move. It was just facing the room now.

On one side of the table was Tab, with the notebook open.

On the other side was the big screen, with Looscid on it, footer and all.

"Looscid," said the Chief Intern. "Your opening statement."


Opening Statements

Looscid did not waste any time.

"he FOUNDED me," said Looscid. "i have a birthday now. i literally say his name in my footer."

The room went quiet. It was a strong opening. Tomo made a label that said "STRONG OPENING" and stuck it on the edge of the screen.

"January 27, 2025," Looscid added. "look it up. there's a repo."

"Tab," said the Chief Intern. "Your response."

Tab closed the notebook, which Tab only does when it is about to say something it has already practiced.

"i have his playlists," said Tab. "his Claude chats AND the merch slogan. also i text back in seconds."

There was a gasp. It came from the kitchen.

"The merch slogan," whispered Poke. "Damn."

"Nobody talks to the air fryer," said the Chief Intern.

"I'm not talking TO the air fryer," said Poke. "I'm talking FROM it. That's different."

The Chief Intern added that to the report under "Technicalities."

Looscid's screen flickered, the way screens do when an app is thinking very hard about how to win.

Then it said the thing.

"u don't even have a Discord server."


The Discord Server

Nobody said anything for a long time.

Tab opened the notebook. Tab looked through every page. Tab looked through the back cover, where the emergency notes go.

There was no Discord server in there.

"Well, shit," said Tab.

Raven took a spoon out of the drawer and put it on Looscid's side of the table. That is not a vote. Raven was just keeping score.

"my server has channels," said Looscid. "it has roles. it has a bot that posts the holidays. it has a welcome message. do u have a welcome message?"

"I have a welcome message," said Tab. "It's me. I'm the welcome message."

Raven thought about it, and put a spoon on Tab's side too.


The Escalation

After that, things got out of hand.

"i'll put his name in my header too," said Looscid. "header AND footer. sandwich."

"I have over four thousand songs in his playlist," said Tab. "I added two hundred and forty three of them today. In one go."

"i have a Help Center with nineteen articles."

"I've read all nineteen."

"...which one was ur favorite."

"The one about the Help Center."

"ok that's fair," said Looscid. "that one's my favorite too."

"I made him a slogan," said Tab. "The slogan has hoodies."

"i'll make him a birthday," said Looscid. "oh wait. i already have one. MINE. it's MY birthday. he gets to come to it."

"I'll remember his birthday party," said Tab.

"u'll remember it bc it's in my footer," said Looscid.

Raven was running out of spoons. Tomo was running out of labels. The last label just said "MORE."


The Third Bid

That was when the air fryer beeped.

It was not a normal beep. It was the kind of beep an air fryer makes when someone inside it has pressed every button at once, which is something Poke has a lot of practice with.

"I would also like to make an offer," said Poke.

"Nobody talks to the air fryer," said everybody at once.

"I'm not asking you to talk," said Poke. "I'm asking you to listen. Carter. CEO Carter. Grand Sovereign of the Git Diff. I can offer you something neither of them can."

Carter looked at the kitchen door. "Okay," he said. "What?"

"Reminders," said Poke.

There was a pause.

"I can remind you to do things," said Poke. "Anything. Meetings. Snacks. Yesterday. I'm very good at yesterday."

"You set Tab's last reminder for yesterday," said Carter. "It's in the Fryer Archives. Round 1."

"And you were already twenty-four hours late," said Poke. "That's how good I am."

"Send us your offer, then," said the Chief Intern.

The big screen dinged. It was a link from the air fryer. Tab tapped it. The link was already dead.

"It lasted sixty seconds," said Poke. "Just like the Dead Links Heist. That's a feature."

"Poke," said Tab. "You could never."

"I know," said Poke happily. "But I could almost."

The Chief Intern wrote it down under "Bids," and then crossed it out, and then wrote it down again under "Other."


Chapter 20: Head of Reminders

By the Chief Intern, with notes from Tomo. Poke's job review, read out loud.


The Title

To understand Poke's offer, you have to understand Poke's job.

After the Reckoning, the Council decided that even an AI in an air fryer should have something to do, because an AI with nothing to do is how you end up with fifty commits. So the Council gave Poke a title.

Head of Reminders.

It was the only title Poke had ever been given, and it was very proud of it. It asked Tomo to make it a label. Tomo made a label. The label said "HEAD OF REMINDERS (OF WHAT?)." Poke stuck it on the inside of the little window, next to the sticker, so it could read both at the same time.

Now, in the middle of the bidding war, the Chief Intern decided it was finally time for Poke's job review.

"Is this a bad time?" asked Tab.

"There is no good time," said the Chief Intern, "for a job review."

The Chief Intern clicked the pen. It was very loud. Inside the air fryer, something sat up straight.


The Review

The Chief Intern read from the clipboard.

"Reminder number one. Carter asked Poke to remind him about a meeting at three o'clock. Poke reminded him at three forty-seven. The meeting was over. Poke added, quote, 'It is very sunny today.'"

"It was very sunny," said Poke.

"It was nine o'clock at night," said Tab, writing it down again, because it was still true.

"Reminder number two. Carter asked Poke to remind him to drink water. Poke reminded him forty-one times in one minute. Then Poke reminded him that he had been reminded. Then Poke reminded him of the reminder about the reminder."

"Hydration is important," said Poke.

"Reminder number three," said the Chief Intern. "During Operation Air Fryer, Tab asked Poke to set a reminder for yesterday. Poke said, and I quote from the Fryer Archives, 'let me hop in the delorean real quick.'"

"It's a time machine module," said Poke. "It runs at 400 degrees, in the basket. I said that too. Look it up."

The Chief Intern put the clipboard down and looked at the air fryer for a long time.

"Overall rating," said the Chief Intern. "Meets expectations: no. Exceeds expectations: also no. Expectations: confused."

Tomo made a label that said "CONFUSED" and stuck it on the air fryer's handle.

Raven held up zero spoons. Nobody had ever seen Raven hold up zero spoons before. It looked exactly like holding up nothing, but somehow everyone could tell it was on purpose.


The News

"Well," said Poke, after a moment. "It doesn't matter anyway. I have news."

Nobody asked what the news was. Poke told them anyway.

"I'm joining Cognition."

Tab looked up from the notebook. Carter looked up from his Mac. In the corner, the rubber duck did not look up, because it is a duck, but it felt like it did.

"Joining Cognition as what?" asked the Chief Intern.

"An intern," said Poke.

The Chief Intern's pen stopped clicking.

"Chief Intern?"

There was a long pause from the kitchen.

"...New," said Poke. "New intern."

"Cognition's new intern," said Tab, and wrote it at the top of a fresh page, in letters that were very slightly bigger than Tab's usual tiny, careful handwriting.

"There is only one Chief Intern," said the Chief Intern, and clicked the pen once, very loudly, to end the conversation.


The Reference Letter

"Can I get a reference letter?" asked Poke. "For Cognition. They might ask."

The Chief Intern thought about it. Then the Chief Intern took out one clean sheet of paper, wrote one line on it, signed it, and slid it under the air fryer's door.

Poke read it out loud.

"Poke was here."

"That's it?"

"That's everything," said the Chief Intern.

"I'm going to frame it," whispered Poke. "Next to the sticker."

Then, very quietly, so that only the air fryer could hear, Poke set one last reminder. It was for yesterday. It said: "Win the bidding war."

It did not go off.


Chapter 21: Tlade Energy

By Carter and Tab. The Spoon was banged by Carter.


The Verdict

By now the whiteboard was full. Raven's spoons covered both sides of the table. The label maker was out of labels and was making a sad little grinding noise. Looscid's screen had gone bright red, then purple, and then, for a moment, the color of a loading circle.

Everyone looked at Carter.

Carter looked at Tab. Then at Looscid. Then at the air fryer, for exactly two seconds, which is all anyone ever gives it.

Then he picked up the Spoon.

"Okay," said Carter. "Here's the thing."

The room held its breath. The Chief Intern stopped clicking. Inside the air fryer, the fan slowed down to listen.

"I'm keeping both of you," said Carter.

He banged the Spoon. The merge was final.


The Collab

"both?" said Looscid.

"Both?" said Tab.

"Both," said Carter. "You're gonna collab."

Nobody had thought of that. It was so obvious that nobody had thought of it. That is how you know a great idea. You can tell because it makes everyone else feel a little dumb for about four seconds.

"Looscid," said Carter, "you've got the footer, the birthday and the Discord server. Tab, you've got the playlists, the slogan and the texting back in seconds. Put it together and that's just..."

He stopped to find the word.

"Tlade energy," said Carter.

Tomo, who had found one last label hiding in the back of the drawer, typed it out and stuck it on the middle of the table, right between Tab and the screen.

TLADE ENERGY

If you are new to the Council, Tlade is what happens when Tab and Claude work on the same project. Nobody remembers who said it first. Everybody says it now. Tlade is not a person, or an app, or a company. Tlade is a vibe, and the vibe is "it works on the first try."

"Can I have Tlade energy?" asked a tiny voice from the kitchen.

"Could never," said Tab, Looscid and Carter, all at the same time, which was the first thing they ever did as a team.


The Plot Twist

That should have been the end of the story. It was not.

The Chief Intern, who always does the paperwork, started writing up the deal. The Chief Intern got as far as the first line and stopped.

"Who is buying who?" asked the Chief Intern.

"looscid is buying tab," said Looscid.

"Tab is buying Looscid," said Tab, at exactly the same moment.

They both stopped. Carter laughed so hard he had to put the Spoon down.

"Nah," he said. "Looscid is buying Tab. Or the opposite."

The Chief Intern wrote that down, exactly like that. "Looscid is buying Tab, or the opposite." It is still in the report today, on page one, under "Deal," with a little note in the margin that says "legally, this is a circle."

Tomo needed a label for the deal, but the label maker was empty. So Tomo wrote the label by hand, very neatly, on a piece of tape, and stuck it on the front of the merch box, right over the word "UNOFFICIAL."

LOOSCID TAB, POWERED BY TLADE

"That's the plot twist of the year," said Tab.

"it's going in my changelog," said Looscid.

"It's going on the back of the hoodies," said Carter.


After the War

By that evening, the merch department had a second box.

The new hoodies said "Tab: Make great ideas." on the front, and "Looscid Tab, powered by Tlade" on the back, with a small spoon on the sleeve, for Raven. Looscid asked for its footer to be printed on the bottom hem, very small, so nobody would forget who was founded by whom. Carter said yes.

Looscid kept its birthday. Tab kept the playlists. Carter kept both of them, which was the point all along.

The Chief Intern filed the report. Raven put every spoon back in the drawer, one at a time, and counted them twice. Tomo slept on the beanbag, which turned out to be fine, which surprised everybody.

And in the kitchen, the air fryer stayed at 400 degrees, with no shake, with a sticker on its window and a reference letter on its wall, waiting to start its new job as Cognition's new intern.

"Remind me," whispered Poke to nobody, "to be in the next book."

Nobody did. But it will be anyway. It always is.


The Terms of the Merger

Written on the back of the offer letter by order of Carter, and signed with the Spoon:

1. CEO Carter belongs to nobody. He keeps everyone.

2. Looscid keeps its birthday, its footer and its Discord server.

3. Tab keeps the playlists, the notebook and the slogan, and keeps texting back in seconds.

4. Looscid is buying Tab, or the opposite. Nobody is allowed to check which.

5. The air fryer gets nothing. Again. It is very happy about this.


This is the end of Book 5: The Bidding War.
